# Changelog

## 0.5.0

First public release.

- Russian interface when the game is in Russian (the key names too).
- A dimension button: look at the Overworld, the Nether or the End from any of them; the
  Nether and Overworld coordinates of the place under the pointer are shown.
- A waypoint list (L) with search, nearest first; click to go there, right-click for the menu.
- The biome under the pointer, other players on the map, a settings page in Options > Mods.
- Colour picker in the waypoint window; Go to coordinates (J).

- Self-writing full screen map, kept per world, dimension and cave layer.
- Smooth drag and zoom; far zoom blurs instead of flickering.
- Cave layers (layered or full) with a Top Y slider, -64 to 319.
- Waypoints: add, rename, 16 colours, three shapes, teleport, delete, hide; in-world
  markers; a waypoint where you die.
- Go to coordinates, chunk grid, your trail, loading chunks shown, PNG export.
- Nine rebindable keys, in Key Binds under *World Map*, with Reset and shared-key marks.
- A single player world is kept under its name; maps made before that are moved to the
  first world opened.
